Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our programmes

What qualifications are needed to join O-Level?

Students joining Senior One need a Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) certificate. Transfers into Senior Two-Four are assessed individually by our admissions office.

How do students choose an A-Level combination?

Combinations are guided by a student's UCE results and career interests. Our academic counsellors meet with every Senior Four student to recommend the best-fit combination before Senior Five.

Do you offer boarding facilities?

Yes, we offer secure boarding facilities for both O-Level and A-Level students, alongside day-school options for students within Mukono.

When does the academic year begin?

Our academic year follows the Ministry of Education calendar, typically beginning early February and running in three terms.
