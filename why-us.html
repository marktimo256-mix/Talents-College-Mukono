Our Track Record

For over 19 years, Talents College Mukono has grown into a school of choice for families across Mukono and beyond. Today we are home to more than 850 students, guided by a team of over 35 qualified teachers who bring genuine commitment to every classroom.

Our consistent focus on strong UCE and UACE results, paired with a caring, well-supervised campus, is why so many families choose to stay with us from Senior One right through to Senior Six — and why so many more are referred to us by parents who trust what we do.
